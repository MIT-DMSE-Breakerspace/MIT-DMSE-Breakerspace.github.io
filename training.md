# Get Trained

Start with the **one-time online training and record**, then attend hands-on training for each instrument you want to use. Your trainer will help you get set up for independent use.

## In-Person Instrument Training {#quick-actions}

Train on each instrument before reserving or using it independently. Standard sessions last one hour, with up to three participants. If no listed session works for you or you need help registering, email [dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu](mailto:dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu) with the instrument and several times that work for you.

<div id="2-register-for-training"></div>

{% include training-sessions.html %}

Public calendar sessions currently prioritize MIT undergraduates. MIT faculty, staff, and other community members interested in training should contact [dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu](mailto:dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu) directly to discuss their needs.

Please cancel your registration if your plans change so another student can take your place. There is no penalty for missing a session or registering again.
