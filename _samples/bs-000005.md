description: Plastilina modeling clay is used throughout the Breakerspace to fixture
  and support samples for X-ray diffraction (XRD), optical microscopy, and, in some
  cases, scanning electron microscopy (SEM). Because the modeling clay contributes
  to the measured XRD pattern, samples mounted in Plastilina may exhibit a background
  contribution from the mounting material. The accompanying reference XRD dataset
  can be used to identify and, where appropriate, account for or subtract the Plastilina
  contribution during data analysis.
