# Lab Assistant Handbook

Breakerspace Lab Assistants provide drop-in help, deliver the instrument trainings for which they are qualified, support scheduled lab activities, and help keep the lab ready for safe use. This page states the shared expectations for the role and links to the detailed procedures.

## Weekly Expectations

* Ideally offer at least **one instrument training per week**.
* Staff at least **three additional Lab Assistant on duty hours per week**.
* Do not average more than **10 paid hours per week** without clearing the additional time with the lab manager.
* Submit hours through the MIT payroll system. A separate payroll email is not required.
* Keep your on-duty hours and training events accurate in LibCal; follow the [calendar scheduling guide]({% link _staff-training/scheduling.md %}).

## Start And End Of A Shift

Make a quick status check of the lab at both the beginning and end of the shift:

* Check the area around each unoccupied instrument. Leave it tidy and free of samples.
* Check that the sample-preparation area is clean and organized.
* Check commonly used supplies, including gloves, tape, sample stubs, Kimwipes, and canned air.
* Notify the lab manager through the staff Slack channel or `dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu` when supplies need reordering or when something is missing, damaged, or not in its expected state.

Do not interrupt an occupied instrument or discard an unidentified sample. Ask in the staff channel when ownership or the correct disposition is unclear.

## Helping Users

Offer drop-in help on a first-come, first-served basis. Help within your training and authority; if you do not know an answer, say so and ask the staff group rather than guessing about instrument limits, sample compatibility, access, or safety.

Scheduled subjects and whole-lab activities may reserve space farther in advance than individual users. That calendar priority does not create priority over ordinary users outside the reserved period. Once users are working in the lab under normal reservations and access rules, apply the same policy to everyone: coursework does not displace an existing reservation or an independent project.

Only deliver an instrument training after completing the per-instrument process in [Lab Assistant Onboarding]({% link _staff-training/trainer-readiness.md %}). Follow [Run An Instrument Training]({% link _staff-training/run-a-training.md %}) from scheduling through closeout.

## Absence And Coverage

If you cannot attend scheduled hours:

1. Remove your `Lab Assistant on duty` hours from the calendar.
2. Remove a training event if nobody has registered.
3. If people have registered, seek coverage from another qualified trainer.
4. If coverage is unavailable, cancel the event and notify the registrants that they need to reschedule.

Make the calendar change as soon as you know you cannot attend. Do not leave public hours or training availability posted without a staff member who can cover them.

## Escalation And Safety

Use either the private lab staff channel in Slack or `dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu` for routine questions, supply needs, instrument problems, scheduling issues, and anything requiring staff attention. The lab manager monitors both; another staff member may answer when they can resolve the issue.

For an emergency or immediate danger, follow the [Breakerspace safety response](https://breakerspace.mit.edu/safety.html#emergency-or-immediate-danger) rather than waiting for email or Slack. Report injuries, near misses, equipment damage, and unsafe conditions even when the immediate problem appears resolved.

## Check Your Onboarding

Review [Lab Assistant Onboarding]({% link _staff-training/trainer-readiness.md %}) when joining the staff or taking on a new instrument. Account access and trainer qualification are separate: having LibCal, Slack, Dropbox, repository, workstation, or physical access does not by itself qualify someone to train others.

If an expected account, physical access, safety orientation, supervised practice, or qualification step was missed, stop and ask the lab manager to complete the process. Individual progress and qualification records belong in the private staff system, not on this site.
