# Run An Instrument Training

Use this checklist only for an instrument on which you are qualified to train.

## 1. Schedule It

* Create the event from the correct instrument-training template.
* Select **No Location** so the event does not block every instrument.
* Make a separate reservation for the instrument's **seat** for the full training period.
* Confirm that the public calendar shows the event and that only the training instrument is unavailable.

See [Calendar Event Scheduling]({% link _staff-training/scheduling.md %}) for the exact LibCal workflow.

## 2. Prepare

Before the session:

* Check registrations and any attendee notes.
* Review the instrument's trainer session guide, public operating page, and Quick Guide.
* Confirm that the instrument is working, the training sample and supplies are ready, and the area is tidy.
* Allow time before the event for setup and after it for user and instrument closeout.

## 3. Deliver The Training

* Follow the instrument's trainer session guide, including the [lab safety orientation]({% link _staff-training/lab-safety-orientation.md %}), and use the approved training sample.
* Give every attendee a meaningful hands-on role.
* Cover the instrument's limits, stop conditions, cleanup, data handling, and where to find help.
* Do not guess about sample compatibility, safety, or instrument limits. Pause and ask staff when unsure.

## 4. Close Out

Before attendees leave:

* Confirm who attended and each person's Kerberos username.
* Tell attendees that staff will add them to the instrument's Moira list.
* Point them to the public instrument page, reservations, `#breakerspace-help`, and `dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu`.
* Explain that physical tap access is processed separately by the lab manager after the one-time Qualtrics record is complete.

After attendees leave:

* Add every attendee to the Moira list named in that instrument's trainer guide.
* Invite new users to the Breakerspace Slack workspace.
* Make sure new users know how to create an MIT Dropbox for Business account and request the Breakerspace team when needed.
* Leave the instrument and surrounding area in their expected state.
* Report instrument, supply, guide, or training problems in the private staff Slack channel or to `dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu`.

If the instrument is not working, a sample is not approved, someone is hurt, or you cannot complete the session safely, stop the affected work and involve staff. Follow the [public safety response]({% link safety.md %}) for injuries, damage, near misses, or immediate hazards.
