# Lab Assistant Onboarding

Use this checklist when joining the Breakerspace staff. If anything was missed, ask the lab manager to complete it. Account or instrument access does not by itself qualify you to train others.

## Staff Setup

Confirm that you have:

* [ ] Physical access to the instrument lab and lounge.
* [ ] LibCal administrative access.
* [ ] Membership in the Breakerspace staff Moira list.
* [ ] Access to the Breakerspace Dropbox team.
* [ ] Access to the Breakerspace Slack workspace and private staff channel.
* [ ] Access to the website repository on GitHub.
* [ ] Completed the required lab-safety orientation.

Create an [MIT Dropbox for Business account](https://ist.mit.edu/dropbox/business) before requesting team membership if you do not already have one.

## Learn The Role

* [ ] Read the [Lab Assistant handbook]({% link _staff-training/handbook.md %}).
* [ ] Read the [calendar scheduling guide]({% link _staff-training/scheduling.md %}).
* [ ] Review the [lab safety orientation]({% link _staff-training/lab-safety-orientation.md %}) and locate the safety equipment in person.
* [ ] Learn how to use WebMoira, invite users to Slack, and respond to Dropbox team requests.
* [ ] Confirm scheduled shifts and how to report hours.

## Become Qualified To Train

Qualification is separate for each instrument. Before offering a training:

1. Complete the instrument's user training and gain enough independent experience to recognize ordinary problems.
2. Read its public operating page, Quick Guide, and trainer session guide.
3. Observe a qualified trainer deliver the complete session and closeout.
4. Deliver a complete practice session while a qualified trainer or the lab manager observes.
5. Confirm that your qualification was recorded in the private staff channel.

Only schedule training for instruments on which you have completed all five steps. See [Run An Instrument Training]({% link _staff-training/run-a-training.md %}) for the working checklist.
