      <div class="quick-guide-sop-map-sop-column quick-guide-sop-map-sop-column-primary">
        <h2 class="quick-guide-sop-map-sop-column-title">Standard Operating Procedure <span>(SOP)</span></h2>

        <section class="quick-guide-sop-map-sop-section">
          <h3><span>1</span> Instrument startup</h3>
          <ul>
            <li>Wear nitrile gloves when handling samples, stage plates, objectives, or sample-prep tools.</li>
            <li>Switch the microscope on.</li>
            <li>Log on to the instrument workstation using your MIT Kerberos.</li>
            <li>Clear the stage of any samples or other materials.</li>
            <li>Start the DSX software and log on as Guest with no password.</li>
            <li>Acknowledge that it is safe for the stage and head to move.</li>
            <li>Use the manual focusing knob to lower the microscope stage.</li>
            <li>Load or change objectives if needed.</li>
            <li>Lower the microscope head into the tilt position using the button on the console or the software button.</li>
          </ul>
        </section>

        <section class="quick-guide-sop-map-sop-section">
          <h3><span>2</span> Operation</h3>
          <ul>
            <li>Place the sample on the stage.</li>
            <li>Remove gloves before using the keyboard, mouse, or instrument workstation.</li>
            <li>Use the manual focusing knob to bring the sample into rough focus.</li>
            <li>Use the joystick to move the stage and position the sample area under observation.</li>
            <li>Fine-tune focus by moving the zoom head with the console buttons, focus wheel, or software buttons.</li>
            <li>Characterize the sample as appropriate for your goals (see detailed operating instructions).</li>
            <li>Verify that files are saved where you intend.</li>
            <li>Wear gloves again before unloading or handling samples.</li>
          </ul>
        </section>

        <section class="quick-guide-sop-map-sop-section">
          <h3><span>3</span> Instrument shutdown</h3>
          <ul>
            <li>Save and copy any data you need.</li>
            <li>Remove your sample from the stage.</li>
            <li>Close the DSX software.</li>
            <li>Click yes to exit the microscope system and retract the head.</li>
            <li>Once the software fully closes, switch the microscope off.</li>
            <li>Log out of Windows.</li>
            <li>Place the dust cover on the microscope.</li>
            <li>Leave the stage, sample area, and workstation clean.</li>
          </ul>
        </section>
      </div>

      <div class="quick-guide-sop-map-sop-column quick-guide-sop-map-sop-column-secondary">
        <aside class="quick-guide-sop-map-critical-rule">
          <h2>Protect the objective and sample</h2>
          <p>Before software startup, objective changes, tilt, or stitched/3D motion, make sure the sample and stage area have enough clearance.</p>
          <p>Wear gloves for samples and microscope hardware; remove gloves before using the keyboard, mouse, or workstation.</p>
        </aside>

        <aside class="quick-guide-sop-map-before-leaving">
          <h2>Before you leave</h2>
          <ul>
            <li>Data saved and copied</li>
            <li>Sample removed</li>
            <li>Stage, sample area, and workstation clean</li>
            <li>DSX closed; Windows logged out</li>
            <li>Head retracted; microscope off and covered</li>
          </ul>
        </aside>

        <aside class="quick-guide-sop-map-stop-help">
          <div>
            <h2>Stop and ask staff</h2>
            <p>Collision risk; unstable, sharp, fragile, wet, loose, powdery, odorous, unknown, unusually heavy, or unclear sample; control or connection error; sample shifting during motion; or focus that cannot be reached safely.</p>
