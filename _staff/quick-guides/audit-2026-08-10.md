# Quick Guide Provisional Set Audit — 2026-08-10

Audit of the complete unpublished SOP-and-visual-map prototype set: the FTIR composition reference plus the ten provisional instrument drafts created by the authorized [provisional composition pass](README.md#provisional-placeholder-draft-mode).

**Audited state:** branch `codex/quick-guide-rollout` at commit `384d198`, clean working tree. Read-only review; nothing was edited, staged, committed, or pushed.

**Post-audit source note (2026-08-11):** all eleven prototype pages were migrated to the repository's include-backed Quick Guide contract. The audit's prototype line numbers remain historical references to commit `384d198`; the current HTML bodies now live at `_includes/quick-guides/*-sop-map-prototype.html`, while the top-level Markdown files are thin wrappers.

**Post-audit FTIR note (2026-08-12):** the canonical reconciliation and native-capture follow-up address FTIR BLOCKER-2, BLOCKER-3, MAJOR-5, and MAJOR-6 in the current provisional draft. Three native 1920×1080 captures replace the failed poster frames; the key uses the visible `Col Bkg`, `Col Smp`, `Save`, `System Status`, and `Start Collection` labels; the contact caption follows the reviewed canonical cue; and the callout geometry was repositioned for the selected captures. The original findings and measurements below remain the historical audit record. The FTIR draft is still unpublished and blocked on its Side 1 layout exception, final staff media/callout review, 100% physical proofing, QR scan confirmation, and duplex approval. The cross-instrument header trial also remains unreviewed.

**Scope:** document fidelity, sourcing, placeholder honesty, Level 1 boundaries, cross-instrument contamination, image suitability, and real paginated print behavior. This audit does **not** substitute for operational verification at any instrument, staff technical review, or a physical print proof.

**Status of the set:** every draft correctly remains unpublished and unmistakably incomplete. None is approved for technical completion, publication, printing for use, or installation. Findings below are corrections needed *before* those gates, not permission to open them.

## How To Use This Document

* Work findings in severity order. Each carries the prototype line, the canonical or work-order source line, the required correction, and a confidence level.
* **Do not fix a finding by weakening canonical content.** Several corrections are explicitly blocked from being resolved by shortening an SOP step, shrinking type, narrowing a curriculum, or trimming a canonical instrument identity.
* Findings marked **staff decision** change locked template geometry or unresolved policy. An implementation assistant must not resolve these unilaterally; see [Fixed Versus Replaceable](README.md#fixed-versus-replaceable).
* Section E lists where the FTIR composition itself does not transfer. Those need human design review, not a drafting workaround.
* Re-run the full evidence set in Section F after any change. A word count or scrolled screenshot cannot verify a fixed print layout.

## Summary

**3 blocker, 7 major, 8 minor.**

What the pass got right, and should be preserved in future work:

* All ten drafts are `published: false`, and a normal `bundle exec jekyll build` emits only the three published guides — no prototype leaks into the public build.
* Screen banner and both review badges read **Provisional composition draft — incomplete**; both footers print **Provisional**, not merely *Prototype*.
* **Zero fabricated interfaces.** Across all ten drafts: no `<svg>` callouts, no target outlines, no numbered markers, no control keys, no `<img>` tags, no `TODO`/`TBD`/`FIXME`. Every unverified control appears only as `Label to verify:` inside a placeholder.
* **No cross-instrument contamination.** The Pure draft carries no XL 5–7 mm rule; the XL carries no holder-edge rule; no draft references Moira; no draft invents an XRD workstation sign-in; the hardness sign-in wording matches canonical exactly; Instron keeps handset and Bluehill responsibilities distinct.
* SOP fidelity is high. Phenom XL, Phenom Pure, optical, and Raman are bullet-for-bullet identical to their canonical SOPs; most others differ only in dropped emphasis markup.
* One commit per instrument held. Shared CSS was touched only in the authorization commit, purely additively (+81/−0), leaving locked rules intact.

Highest-risk issues:

1. **A print-layout overlap affects all 11 files, including FTIR** (BLOCKER-1). It degrades the notice stating the sheet does not authorize independent use, and it is invisible in a scrolling viewport.
2. **FTIR's source images cannot print** (BLOCKER-2) — all three are 750×468 video-poster frames.
3. **FTIR's control key names labels the interface does not visibly show** (BLOCKER-3).

Cross-cutting lesson: the failure mode in this set is not invention or contamination — the pass was disciplined about both. It is that **the FTIR composition assumed a short instrument title and a real QR asset**, and neither holds for six of the ten new instruments. See Section E.

## Findings

### BLOCKER-1 — Print overlap corrupts the trained-user notice on all 11 prototypes

* **Instrument:** all 11, including the FTIR reference
* **Prototype:** `quick-guides/ftir-sop-map-prototype.md:32-34` (`quick-guide-sop-map-trained-user-note`) against `:38` (`quick-guide-sop-map-sop-column-title`); the same construct at lines `32-38` in all ten provisional drafts
* **Source:** [`README.md`](README.md) validation gate — "no element crosses a page edge, overlaps, or is clipped in the page renders"; [`../../_staff-training/README.md`](../../_staff-training/README.md#quick-guide-layout-model) — "Verify any print layout by rendering it paginated, never by scrolling it"
* **Explanation:** Measured in `print` media, the notice's bottom edge sits below the SOP heading's top edge on every file: **3.8 px** where the `<h1>` fits one line (FTIR, hardness tester, PSA, UV-Vis, ion mill) and **31.2 px** where it wraps to two (Phenom XL, Phenom Pure, XRD, Instron, optical, Raman). Page renders confirm it: on Instron two lines of the notice are struck through and the word "use" is orphaned; on Phenom XL one line is obscured. The four-line provisional badge compounds the header height. FTIR's 3.8 px is latent today but is the same defect.
* **Required correction: staff decision** on locked header/notice spacing — page size, spacing, and type sizes are **Locked. Do not change.** Do not resolve by shortening a canonical instrument title or shrinking type. Re-render all 11 paginated and re-measure afterward.
* **Confidence:** High — direct geometry measurement, corroborated in three page renders.

## Structural Exceptions — Where The FTIR Composition Does Not Transfer

The FTIR composition was extracted from an instrument with a short name, an approved single-screen software map, and real assets. Six exceptions need human design review. **These are not redesign proposals.**

1. **Long instrument identities break the header.** Phenom XL, Phenom Pure, XRD, Instron, optical, and Raman all wrap the `<h1>` to two lines, turning FTIR's latent 3.8 px collision into a 31.2 px overlap. FTIR never exercised this case. *Needs review: how the locked header accommodates a full canonical instrument identity — noting that shortening the identity conflicts with the source-preflight rule.*
2. **The provisional badge is four lines where FTIR's is two.** "Provisional composition draft — incomplete" occupies far more header height than "Prototype / Review only," compounding exception 1. *Needs review: badge geometry for provisional mode.*
3. **Instron and Raman genuinely need multiple control surfaces.** Both drafts correctly refuse to fake it, so their primary map slot holds a placeholder and no map — the one-map default cannot express their curriculum. *Needs review: the named two-view (Instron) and multi-state (Raman) variants.*
4. **Optical may join them** pending interface capture — five taught functions may not coexist in one legible state.
