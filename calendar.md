# Calendar & Lab Availability

Use this calendar to find and register for instrument training, see when a Breakerspace Lab Assistant will be on duty for drop-in project support, and check for scheduled activities that may limit access to part or all of the instrument lab.

<p class="guidance-note"><strong>Looking for a particular instrument?</strong> Individual instrument reservations are managed separately. An open period on this calendar does not guarantee that an instrument is available; <a href="https://breakerspace.libcal.com/space/168610">check individual instrument availability in LibCal</a>.</p>

## Calendar {#calendar}

Calendar entries use these text labels so their meaning does not depend on color:

* **Training:** Open the event to review the instrument, prerequisites, available places, and registration details.
* **Lab Assistant on duty:** A Breakerspace Lab Assistant will be present for drop-in project questions and ad-hoc support. These hours are not a substitute for required instrument training or reservations.
* **Reserved activity or availability notice:** Teaching, group activities, maintenance, or other scheduled work may affect part or all of the lab. Open the event to see the affected area or instruments.

## Understanding Lab Availability

The Breakerspace instrument lab and lounge are normally open from 8 AM to midnight, seven days/week. **Lab Assistant on duty** entries identify times when drop-in support is available; they are not the lab's opening hours.

Confirmed teaching sessions, organized activities, and maintenance periods will indicate whether the whole lab, one area, or particular instruments are affected. Even when no general availability notice appears, use the individual instrument calendar before planning independent work.
