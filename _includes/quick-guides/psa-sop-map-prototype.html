Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1 Instrument startup

  • Power on the instrument if needed. The instrument may be left powered on, so it is often already on.
  • Log on to the instrument workstation using your MIT Kerberos.
  • Open the Microtrac FLEX software.
  • From the Open Analyzer menu, select Sync Analyzer.
  • Wait while the instrument initializes. Keep the sample compartment door closed during initialization.

2 Operation

  • Check the active database and change or create a new one if needed, so your data is saved where you expect.
  • Load a measurement SOP. If no suitable SOP exists, ask lab staff for assistance.
  • Clean loose particles from the sample area (see cleaning the sample area on the canonical page).
  • Load the sample using the appropriate accessory and the selected measurement SOP (see loading a sample on the canonical page).
  • Close the sample compartment door.
  • Run the Auto-Sequence, editing the title, sample ID, and notes as appropriate.
  • Repeat as needed.

3 Instrument shutdown

  • Export data as needed (data is also saved automatically to the active database).
  • Close the FLEX software.
  • Log off the Windows workstation.
  • The instrument may be left powered on.
  • Leave the sample area and work surface clean.

Load dry powder loosely

For the standard shallow tray, use about 1/4 teaspoon of dry powder, keep it behind the line, and spread it along the tray without compressing it.

Clean loose particles before loading. The material must be non-hazardous, dry, free-flowing, safe to aerosolize, and within the canonical size range.

Before you leave

  • Data in the intended active database; needed copy exported
  • Tray/sample path empty; analyzed powder not recoverable
  • Sample area and work surface clean
  • FLEX closed; Windows workstation logged off
  • Instrument left powered on

Stop and ask staff

Uncertain material suitability or safe aerosolization; wet, sticky, oily, hazardous, or reactive material; no suitable measurement SOP; a deep tray seems necessary; or connection, initialization, or run errors remain after the canonical checks.

Review gap

Decision required: Approve the active-database and file/export conventions and confirm the current standard measurement SOP, or retain the canonical ask-staff fallback.
