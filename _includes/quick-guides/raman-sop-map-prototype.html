Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1 Instrument startup

  • Log on to the instrument workstation using your MIT Kerberos.
  • Open the LabSpec 6 software.
  • Confirm the instrument is powered on. It should always be left powered on.
  • Turn the laser emission remote-control power to on if needed. This enables the lasers but does not fire one.

2 Operation

  • Prepare your sample on a glass microscope slide (see compatible materials and sample prep).
  • Lower the microscope stage with the coarse focus knob.
  • Load the slide into the slide holder on the stage.
  • Set the brightfield light source to full brightness.
  • Find approximate focus with the coarse knob, adjusting brightness as needed.
  • Refine the focus by rotating the joystick.
  • Close the doors on the microscope enclosure.
  • Collect Raman spectra as appropriate for your sample (see detailed operating instructions).
  • Repeat as needed.

3 Instrument shutdown

  • Remove your sample from the stage and close the doors.
  • Turn the laser emission remote-control power to off.
  • Save all data. Each spectrum must be selected and saved individually.
  • Close LabSpec 6.
  • Log off the workstation.

Laser enclosure rule

Close the enclosure doors before verifying the laser or collecting, and respect the enclosure, interlock, and key conditions.

If the laser is not visible, recheck the documented door, interlock, and key conditions rather than bypassing anything.

Before you leave

  • Every needed spectrum selected and saved
  • Sample removed
  • Enclosure doors closed
  • Laser emission remote-control power off
  • LabSpec closed; workstation logged off; instrument powered on

Stop and ask staff

Uncertain sample or trained settings; door, interlock, key, camera, or laser problem; calibration that does not pass; weak or absent signal after focus checks; fluorescence; sample damage; or a laser that appears misaligned.

Review gap

Decision required: Verify the laser-safety, interlock, and sample-change sequence and confirm save/export and shutdown on the actual system.
