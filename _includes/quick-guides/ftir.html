DMSE Breakerspace · Trained-user reference

Nicolet iS5 FTIR Spectrometer

Collect a clean ATR spectrum, preserve the data, and leave the crystal ready for the next user.

This guide supports completed hands-on training; it does not replace training. Routine independent use is ATR with approved, non-hazardous samples.

What FTIR shows

FTIR records how a sample absorbs infrared light as its molecular bonds vibrate. The spectrum is a pattern of bands that can support material identification or comparison. With ATR, the signal comes mainly from the material touching the diamond crystal, so clean surfaces and good contact matter.

Choose the routine ATR approach

SampleStarting approach
Flat solidClean face; self-leveling tip; firm contact
PowderSmall, finely ground amount; concave or self-leveling tip
Non-volatile liquidSmall droplet; no pressure tip
Volatile liquidVolatiles cover; ask if odor or exposure is uncertain

Compatible samples

  • Use only non-hazardous materials and approved cleaning solvents.
  • Use powders sparingly and clean the entire crystal plate afterward.
  • Samples must not spill, crumble into the instrument, stain the plate, persistently smell, or damage the diamond.
  • Ask first about sharp, sticky, abrasive, very hard, reactive, odorous, unknown, staining, or difficult-to-clean materials.

Read the spectrum carefully

  • Compare the full pattern with a known reference, database match, control, or related sample.
  • Weak signal often means poor ATR contact.
  • Negative bands can indicate a dirty crystal during background collection.
  • Water vapor, carbon dioxide, solvent, and residue can add features.
  • Complex mixtures such as coffee give combined fingerprints, not a direct concentration of each ingredient.

Stop and ask staff

Do not proceed with an uncertain sample, damaged or contaminated crystal, unfamiliar accessory, persistent connection error, or spectrum that remains abnormal after cleaning and recollecting the background.

Routine ATR trained-user workflow

1 Start and collect a background

  1. Leave the FTIR powered on unless staff have instructed otherwise.
  2. Sign in to Windows using the current instructions provided during training or posted at the instrument, then open OMNIC.
  3. Confirm that the instrument is connected in system status.
  4. Wear nitrile gloves. Remove the ATR cover and inspect the crystal and plate.
  5. Clean the crystal with isopropyl alcohol and a Kimwipe; allow it to dry.
  6. Remove gloves before using the workstation and collect a background with nothing on the crystal.

2 Load the sample

  1. Wear gloves before handling the sample, pressure tower, tip, or crystal area.
  2. Place the sample directly over the diamond using the appropriate approach from Side 1.
  3. For solids or powders, center the pressure tower and tighten until the clutch slips.
  4. For a liquid, use a small droplet; use the volatiles cover when appropriate.
  5. Remove gloves before returning to the computer.

3 Collect and save

  1. Preview the spectrum and check signal strength, baseline, and obvious contamination.
  2. If needed, wear gloves and improve sample contact or clean and recollect the background.
  3. Collect the sample spectrum with a descriptive name.
  4. Select and save every spectrum you need individually. Preserve the native spectrum file before exporting plots or reports.
  5. Repeat the clean/background/load/collect/save cycle when contamination or comparison quality requires it.

4 Clean and close

  1. Save and copy all data you need.
  2. Wear gloves, unload the sample, and remove all visible residue.
  3. Clean the crystal and surrounding plate with isopropyl alcohol and a Kimwipe.
  4. Replace the crystal cover and clamp it in place with the pressure tower.
  5. Remove gloves, close OMNIC, and log out of Windows.
  6. Leave the FTIR powered on and remove samples, wipes, and waste from the area.

Common reasons to stop

  • Sample compatibility or cleaning method is uncertain
  • Crystal is scratched, chipped, stained, or will not come clean
  • Pressure tip or accessory choice is unclear
  • Instrument will not connect or the background remains abnormal

Need help?

Leave the sample unloaded and the crystal clean. Ask a lab assistant, use #breakerspace-help, or email dmse-breakerspace@mit.edu.

Before leaving: spectra saved, sample removed, crystal clean and covered, OMNIC closed, Windows logged out, FTIR left on.
