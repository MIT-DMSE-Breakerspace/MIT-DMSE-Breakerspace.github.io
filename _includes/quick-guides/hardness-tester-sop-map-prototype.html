Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1 Instrument startup

  • Turn on the machine using the red power switch at the back.
  • Start Impressions using the current sign-in instructions provided during training or posted at the instrument.

2 Operation

  • Place your sample on the stage.
  • Find a clean, flat spot using the camera, moving the stage with the joystick or the software.
  • Focus with the 2.5x objective, then switch to 10x and refocus.
  • Click IN FOCUS.
  • Choose a safe scale and test method with SCALE SELECT (see test method selection on the canonical page).
  • Perform hardness testing appropriate for your sample (see detailed operating instructions on the canonical page).

3 Instrument shutdown

  • Confirm you are back in single-point mode (patterns are retained across users).
  • Remove your sample and turn off the workstation.
  • Toggle the power switch at the back of the machine to off.
  • Leave the stage and work area clean.

Scale, indenter, and turret rule

Match the indenter and scale to the sample. If you are not sure which scale is safe for the material, ask staff.

Keep hands clear of the turret, stage, and spindle whenever they move. Changing installed indenters or objectives is staff-only.

Before you leave

  • Needed readings saved with their scales
  • Sample removed
  • Stage and work area clean
  • Workstation turned off
  • Single-point mode restored; tester switched off

Stop and ask staff

Uncertain material, scale, or indenter compatibility; the on-screen turret map does not match the fitted hardware; a machine warning occurs during an indent; the sample cannot sit flat and stable; or an indenter or objective would need to be changed.

Review gap

Decision required: Inventory the fitted indenter and load hardware, record the exact Level 1 Vickers scale and method, and verify the tester's final state on the current machine.

Vickers indent-quality decision

The proposed pair asks whether the indent is square and symmetric and whether the measured diagonals are accepted in the current software state.

Review gap

Decision required: Approve the matched indent-quality pair and verify the software's current warning behavior, including the documented red diagonal box when diagonal difference exceeds 5%.

Level 1 sequence

Select the staff-verified installed Vickers scale and method, focus and position a prepared sample, confirm single-point mode, make one indent, verify the diagonals and quality state, and save the reading with its scale.

Before unloading

Save the reading to the batch list and keep the scale with the value. The final report/export convention still requires staff confirmation.

Review gap

Decision required: Confirm save/export, the exact final tester state, and every installed-hardware, method, control, and quality-state gate. This draft remains blocked for technical completion, publication, printing for use, and installation.
