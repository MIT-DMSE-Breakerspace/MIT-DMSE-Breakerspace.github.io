Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1 Instrument startup

  • Log on to the instrument workstation using your MIT Kerberos.
  • Start the Phenom User Interface software.
  • Wake the instrument if needed.
  • If the instrument does not connect automatically, open Settings / Phenom / Status and connect to the microscope.

2 Operation

  • Wear nitrile gloves when handling samples, stubs, sample holders, stages, and sample-prep tools.
  • Prepare samples externally at the sample prep table.
  • Confirm that the sample is dry, firmly attached, free of loose particles, and below the XL height limit.
  • Use the height-setting dial to set the tallest point of the sample approximately 5-7 mm below the top edge of the XL sample tray: turn the dial 5-7 notches in the direction that lowers the sample unless staff instruct otherwise.
  • Load the sample tray into the instrument.
  • Remove gloves before using the computer.
  • Set the image label and save location.
  • In NavCam, adjust brightness, contrast, and focus until the image is clear enough to navigate the loaded sample.
  • Characterize the sample as appropriate for your goals (see detailed operating instructions).
  • Wear gloves again, unload samples, and leave the tray clean and stored correctly.

3 Instrument shutdown

  • Save and copy any data you need.
  • Close the Phenom software. Press F11 if you need to exit fullscreen view.
  • Log off Windows.
  • The microscope will put itself in standby.

Sample and holder clearance

Confirm that the sample is dry, firmly attached, and free of loose particles. Turn the height-setting dial 5-7 notches in the direction that lowers the sample to establish the required clearance unless staff instruct otherwise.

Wear gloves for samples, stubs, holders, stages, and prep tools; remove gloves before using the computer.

Before you leave

  • Data saved and copied
  • Samples unloaded
  • Tray clean and stored correctly
  • Phenom software closed; Windows logged off
  • Microscope in standby

Stop and ask staff

Uncertain material, loose, wet, shedding, or over-height sample; failed connection; unexpected stage motion; persistent charging, drift, beam damage, outgassing, or poor focus; or an unfamiliar error.

How the screen is organized

  1. 1Slideout panels: blue edge chevrons open and close the top, bottom, and side panels.
  2. 2Capture: the camera button on the left saves an SEM image.
  3. 3Routine controls: magnification, auto brightness/contrast, and focus are grouped at lower left.
  4. 4Modes: Imaging, Gallery, Live EDS/EDS, and Stitching stay on the right. EDS is shown for orientation only; this guide does not teach EDS operation.

Use lower-left controls — not the coarse sliders

Blue (dark in grayscale) = mouse-wheel target.

Left-click runs Auto. A selected Manual mode instead hands that adjustment to the wheel; it is not the recommended routine path. Right-click a lower-right triangle for additional modes.
  1. Brightness/contrast: left-click A for automatic brightness/contrast. Use automatic adjustment; manual brightness or contrast is not the routine student method.
  2. Focus: left-click the focus control for autofocus. If autofocus misses, right-click its triangle and choose Focus in Area.
  3. With the focal-area box visible, use the wheel to rack focus in that box. Left-click the focus icon so F turns blue for fine steps; this hidden fine-focus state is normally needed for best focus.
  4. When the box is sharp, click outside it to apply that focal plane to the full SEM image. Return the blue wheel target to magnification.

Navigate, move, and capture

Start the SEM view zoomed out. Increase magnification gradually, refocus as needed, run auto brightness/contrast, then use the left-rail camera button to capture.

Choose live and capture settings

  • Live/navigation: use Averaging Medium and Scan Size 960×540.
  • Voltage and beam intensity: match the sample and task. Lower voltage emphasizes nearer-surface signal and can help some charging; higher voltage gives deeper/stronger signal but can worsen charging. Choose Low/Image/Point/Map for the task, and reduce intensity if charging, drift, or beam sensitivity appears.
  • Capture: add pixels or averaging only when useful and stable. “Higher quality” settings can produce a worse image on a charging, drifting, or beam-sensitive sample. Refocus and run auto brightness/contrast after changing settings.

Routine path: Load → double-click NavCam target → move to SEM → Live: Medium averaging + Scan Size 960×540 → wheel magnification → auto B/C → autofocus or Focus in Area + F → capture settings → refocus + auto B/C → camera.
