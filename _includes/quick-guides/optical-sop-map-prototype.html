Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1 Instrument startup

  • Wear nitrile gloves when handling samples, stage plates, objectives, or sample-prep tools.
  • Switch the microscope on.
  • Log on to the instrument workstation using your MIT Kerberos.
  • Clear the stage of any samples or other materials.
  • Start the DSX software and log on as Guest with no password.
  • Acknowledge that it is safe for the stage and head to move.
  • Use the manual focusing knob to lower the microscope stage.
  • Load or change objectives if needed.
  • Lower the microscope head into the tilt position using the button on the console or the software button.

2 Operation

  • Place the sample on the stage.
  • Remove gloves before using the keyboard, mouse, or instrument workstation.
  • Use the manual focusing knob to bring the sample into rough focus.
  • Use the joystick to move the stage and position the sample area under observation.
  • Fine-tune focus by moving the zoom head with the console buttons, focus wheel, or software buttons.
  • Characterize the sample as appropriate for your goals (see detailed operating instructions).
  • Verify that files are saved where you intend.
  • Wear gloves again before unloading or handling samples.

3 Instrument shutdown

  • Save and copy any data you need.
  • Remove your sample from the stage.
  • Close the DSX software.
  • Click yes to exit the microscope system and retract the head.
  • Once the software fully closes, switch the microscope off.
  • Log out of Windows.
  • Place the dust cover on the microscope.
  • Leave the stage, sample area, and workstation clean.

Protect the objective and sample

Before software startup, objective changes, tilt, or stitched/3D motion, make sure the sample and stage area have enough clearance.

Wear gloves for samples and microscope hardware; remove gloves before using the keyboard, mouse, or workstation.

Before you leave

  • Data saved and copied
  • Sample removed
  • Stage, sample area, and workstation clean
  • DSX closed; Windows logged out
  • Head retracted; microscope off and covered

Stop and ask staff

Collision risk; unstable, sharp, fragile, wet, loose, powdery, odorous, unknown, unusually heavy, or unclear sample; control or connection error; sample shifting during motion; or focus that cannot be reached safely.

Review gap

Decision required: Document the current save/copy view and confirm the final clearance state before technical completion.
