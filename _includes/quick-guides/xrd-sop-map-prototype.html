Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

1 Instrument startup

  • Make sure at least one position in the sample changer is free (no sample holder in place). Booting with all six positions occupied causes a startup error.
  • Turn on the mains power switch at the rear of the instrument, if needed. This switch is normally left on.
  • Make sure the cover is closed and the interlocks are engaged.
  • Press the power button to switch on the instrument.
  • Turn the HT keyswitch clockwise to switch on the high-tension (HT) generator.

2 Operation

  • Prepare your sample at the sample prep table using the correct holder (see compatible materials and sample prep).
  • Remove the plastic sample-changer cover and place the prepared holder in any free loading position.
  • Replace the plastic sample-changer cover.
  • In the software, select the changer position that matches the physical slot where you placed the holder (see the queue interface).
  • Select a measurement program from the drop-down list.
  • Enter a descriptive sample name and edit the file name as needed.
  • Start the measurement, or click Add to Queue to run it after the current sample.
  • Export results to the networked workstation or a USB drive.
  • Fill out your information in the X-ray safety log book.

3 Instrument shutdown

  • Confirm all data you need has been exported.
  • Press the power button to switch off the instrument.
  • Turn the HT keyswitch counter-clockwise to switch off the HT generator.
  • Remove your sample holders, return them to storage, and leave the sample prep area clean.

X-ray enclosure and changer-cover rule

The Aeris is fully enclosed and interlocked. Never defeat, bypass, or force an interlock, and never open the enclosure during a measurement.

Replace the plastic sample-changer cover, then match the physical slot to the software position before starting.

Before you leave

  • All needed data exported
  • X-ray safety log book completed
  • Instrument power button switched off
  • HT generator switched off
  • Holders stored; prep area clean

Stop and ask staff

Interlock fault, unusual noise, error, damage, unclear holder or material, loose or poorly secured powder, strongly magnetic powder, or no suitable stored program. Leave the enclosure cover closed if a fault appears.
